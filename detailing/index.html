How it works

Waterless? Does that actually work?

Yes. A waterless wash lifts dirt with a lubricating spray and microfiber instead of a hose, so it's safe on paint, clear coat and vinyl, and there's no runoff on your driveway or in your garage. For a cart caked in mud after a trail day, text a photo first and I'll let you know if it needs a rinse before I come out.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as the cart is reachable, I can detail it in the driveway or garage and text you photos when it's done.

Where do you come to?

Nocatee gets scheduled same day when booked by 9 AM, as slots allow. Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, St. Augustine and Jacksonville get the next available day. No trip fee anywhere in the service area.

Can you do maintenance at the same time?

Yes. Pick "Maintenance + detailing" on the booking form and both happen in one trip. Maintenance prices are here.
