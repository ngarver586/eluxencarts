Service FAQ

Good to know

What brands do you work on?

Club Car, EZGO, Yamaha, and the newer brands like ICON, Evolution, EPIC, Royal EV, Bintelli, Denago and Kandi. Electric and gas.

Do I need to be home?

Not for maintenance or detailing, as long as the cart is reachable and the key is left where we agree. For detailing I need access to an outdoor water spigot.

How does same-day work in Nocatee?

Book by 9 AM and I'll come out that afternoon when a slot is open. Same-day slots are limited each day, so the earlier the better. Annual Care Plan visits are scheduled in advance instead.

What happens when you pick up my cart?

We walk around it together and I photograph it from every side, so its condition is on record before it leaves your driveway. You sign off on the photos, and I send you a copy.

Can you make my cart faster?

Golf carts in Florida are limited to 20 mph. Going faster makes it a low-speed vehicle that has to be titled, registered and insured. I'll tune golf carts within that limit, and work on titled LSVs to their rated speed.

Do you service carts you didn't sell?

Yes. Carts bought from Eluxen get free pickup and return and first priority on the schedule.
