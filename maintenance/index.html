Good to know

How it works

Where do you come to?

Nocatee gets scheduled same day when booked by 9 AM, as slots allow. Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, St. Augustine and Jacksonville get the next available day. No trip fee anywhere in the service area.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as the cart is reachable and the key is left where we agree. You'll get the condition report by text when I'm done.

What brands do you work on?

Club Car, EZGO, Yamaha, and the newer brands like ICON, Evolution, EPIC, Royal EV, Bintelli, Denago and Kandi. Electric and gas.

What if you find something wrong?

It goes on the condition report with a written quote. Nothing beyond the maintenance list gets done without your approval. Small fixes often happen on the spot; anything needing a lift or a bench gets picked up and returned.

Can you detail it on the same visit?

Yes. Pick "Maintenance + detailing" on the booking form and both happen in one trip. Detailing prices are here.
