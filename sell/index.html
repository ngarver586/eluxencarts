Selling FAQ

Good to know

Why sell to a dealer instead of listing it myself?

You can usually get a little more selling private party, if you're willing to field a few dozen messages, meet strangers, take payment safely and handle the title. Selling to me trades some of that top dollar for one text thread, one pickup, and a done deal. Plenty of people decide that's worth it.

Do I need the title?

If your cart was titled as an LSV, yes, I'll need the title at pickup. Most golf carts aren't titled in Florida; for those a bill of sale and your ID cover it. If you're not sure, tell me and I'll sort it out.

What if it doesn't run?

I'll still look at it. Dead lithium packs, controller faults and chargers are my bread and butter, so a cart that won't move is often worth more to me than you'd think. Just be up front about it.

How does a trade-in work?

I make you an offer on your cart, and it comes straight off the price of the cart you're buying or the build you're planning. You see both numbers before you decide.

How fast can you pick it up?

Usually within a few days of agreeing on a price, often sooner in Nocatee. I bring the paperwork and payment with me.
