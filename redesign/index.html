FAQ

Common questions

Can I see a cart in person before buying?

Yes. Viewings are by appointment, 7 days a week. Text or call to set a time.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is free across Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, St. Augustine, and the Jacksonville area, and includes a full walkthrough of your cart. We also deliver across Florida and Georgia for a delivery fee.

Can I add upgrades before delivery?

Yes. Any cart can be lifted, converted to lithium, or fitted with lighting, sound, paint, or new seats before it's delivered. Upgrades bundled with a cart purchase get preferred pricing.

What's checked before a cart is listed?

A full mechanical and electrical inspection: batteries tested, brakes serviced, cosmetics corrected, then a full detail.

Do you buy golf carts?

Yes. Text a few photos with the year, make, and battery type and we'll get back to you.
