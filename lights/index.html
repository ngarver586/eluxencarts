Good to know

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as the cart is reachable, we can install in the driveway or garage and text you photos when it's done.

Where do you come to?

Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, St. Augustine and Jacksonville. We install at your home or business.

Holiday lights or permanent LED?

Holiday lights are the Christmas look for parade season and come off in January. Permanent LED is hard-wired and stays on the cart all year. Not sure which fits? Text a photo of your cart and we'll tell you what works.

What happens to the holiday lights after the season?

We'll take them down in January for $49, or free if you're on an Annual Care Plan. With Parade Ready + Power, the four USB charging ports stay on the cart.

Will it work on my cart?

We work on all makes and models, gas and electric. If your cart has no 12V power or USB port, the Power add-on takes care of it.
